HELLO, GROWN-UPS.

A little less “have to”.
A little more “my turn”.

Learnoids brings spelling, times tables and division together with creatures children can make their own.

FROM A FAMILY IDEA

Made for the little
moments of learning.

Learnoids began with Mark Atkin looking for a more enjoyable way to help his children practise their homework. Spelloids grew around the weekly spelling list. Multiploids brings that same sense of ownership and encouragement to times tables. Divoids takes division to Planet Moz, with a browser playtest ready to try.

The aim is simple: focused practice, a kind response to mistakes and a little creature they are proud to create.

◎

Keep the focus

Choose the words or tables that matter this week. Each app has its own learning activities and rewards.

♡

Keep it kind

Short activities and chances to try again support practice. Whole-list spelling tests use one attempt to record a clear result.

⌂

Keep it on the device

Progress and creations are stored locally. No Learnoids account, public leaderboard or advertising profile.

Aa

Check the words together

Photo reading and the offline glossary are helpers. A grown-up should review the spelling list, meanings and sentences before handing over.

Some useful things to know.

Are the apps free?+
The Divoids browser playtest is free to try. Spelloids and Multiploids are preparing for free App Store launches. This website takes no payments. Public App Store links will appear when available.
Can I use them with more than one child?+
The apps support separate local learner profiles. Use a nickname rather than a child’s full name. Check you have selected the right profile before changing a spelling set or mission.
Do you send photos or children’s work to an AI service?+
The current apps do not send spelling photos, drawings, sentences or practice answers to an online AI service. Spelloids reads photos and prepares any missing speech on the device.
What happens if we delete the app?+
Locally saved progress may be lost. Updating over the existing installation is different from deleting it. Browser data and installed-app data are separate, and Learnoids does not provide cloud recovery.
Can our school use Learnoids?+
We welcome enquiries from schools. The apps are family practice tools and currently have no teacher dashboard, class accounts or school data-sharing system. Please contact us before proposing a class-wide setup.
Is Learnoids endorsed by a school or the government?+
No. Spelloids includes words drawn from England’s spelling curriculum alongside additional words. Learnoids is an independent project and does not claim Department for Education or school endorsement.
