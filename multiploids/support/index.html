Support for Multiploids: Times Tables, for ages 5–11.

Email support@bluffle.co.uk ↗
My rocket hasn’t launched. What is left to do?+
Check the five preparation activities for that numbered rocket. Once they are ready, complete the final launch challenge: all twelve facts for the table, answered correctly in a random order.
What is Cosmo Hop?+
Cosmo Hop is a mixed-table streak challenge alongside the numbered rocket missions.
Why didn’t launching my rocket add another Starbase game?+
Two games are included at the start. Another is added for each different numbered rocket launched; repeating a number already launched does not add a new game.
How do badges and suits work?+
Practice earns customisation badges, including a vivid rainbow suit. Answering 20 different practice questions earns a special weekly suit.
Does the app need internet or an account?+
Core learning and built-in speech work offline in the installed app. There are no learner accounts or device syncing. Downloading the app and opening external support or privacy pages require internet.
Can I move progress from a browser or another device?+
Progress is saved locally. Browser sessions, installed apps and different devices do not automatically share it.
How do I keep our progress when updating?+
Install updates over the existing app. Avoid deleting it or clearing stored data unless you intend to remove progress. Any device backups depend on your Apple settings; Learnoids does not provide cloud recovery.
I cannot hear the voice.+
Check the app’s sound setting and your device’s media volume. Built-in speech is included with the installed app. If it remains silent, email us with the app version and device model.

Still need a hand?

Email support@bluffle.co.uk with the version, device and what happened. Please remove children’s full names and personal information from any screenshots.

The app is free, with no advertising, in-app purchases or third-party analytics. Learner progress stays on the device.
