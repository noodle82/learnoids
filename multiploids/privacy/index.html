APP PRIVACY NOTICE

Multiploids & privacy.

How the current Multiploids app handles learning information, in plain language.

Last updated 23 November 2026

At a glance

Chosen tables, practice answers, mission progress and character choices are kept locally on the device. There is no Learnoids login, public profile, chat, advertising or tracking SDK in this version.

Read the little-person version →

Who runs Multiploids?

Learnoids is operated by Mark Atkin, who is responsible for the personal information described here. For support, privacy questions or a rights request, email support@bluffle.co.uk. Please contact us as a grown-up and avoid sending children’s identifying details.

Information kept on your device

The app stores information needed to remember your chosen learning activities and progress. Use a nickname rather than a child’s full name.

  • Learner nickname, the Multiploid’s name and its appearance, voice and settings.
  • Selected times tables, practice answers, mastered facts and rocket-system progress.
  • Mission and launch records, Cosmo Hop streaks, Starbase game progress, customisation badges and weekly suit progress.
  • Activity state and locally cached app assets, including recorded speech.

This information is used to provide the requested learning activities, save local progress and show rewards and results. Our legitimate interest is operating useful learning tools while minimising the information involved and keeping learning records on your device. It is not used to make advertising profiles or decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

What leaves the device?

The current app does not send learning answers, learner profiles, character choices to a Learnoids server. We have no remote dashboard from which we can view that work. There are no learner accounts or automatic Learnoids cloud sync between devices or between the browser and installed app.

Voices and hands-on activities

Multiploids: Times Tables version 1.0.0 is free, with no advertising, in-app purchases or third-party analytics. Core learning activities and built-in speech work offline in the installed app.

Multiploids plays recorded speech from its app files. It does not need to send answers to an online speech service. Voice selection changes character playback; the app does not record or clone the child’s voice. Counting, drawing and building activities are handled on the device.

Website hosting, Apple and support

If you use a browser version, its host receives the technical requests needed to deliver app files, including IP and request information. This is distinct from uploading learning records. The installed app includes its own assets; downloading and updating through Apple are covered by Apple’s services.

Apple may process App Store, TestFlight, device-backup and diagnostic information under your Apple settings and its privacy policy. If you choose to submit beta feedback, diagnostics or a support email, information included in that report may reach us and be used to investigate the issue. Please remove unnecessary children’s information first.

The shared website notice and support-email notice explain hosting, recipients, lawful bases, retention and international processing. No data is sold and there is no third-party advertising or analytics SDK in the app version described here.

Keeping or removing local information

Local records remain while the relevant app or browser data is retained, unless you remove them through available app settings. Starting another spelling set or mission may archive earlier achievements rather than erase them.

Deleting the installed app or clearing the browser app’s website data can remove local progress and cached files. Offloading an iOS app may keep its data. A device or cloud backup may retain copies according to your backup settings; Learnoids does not operate that backup or provide its own cloud recovery. Update over an existing installation to keep local data where supported. See support before removing data.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or erase personal information we hold, restrict its use, or receive a portable copy where the right applies. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. If we ever rely on consent for an optional activity, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

These rights belong to the child where the information concerns the child. We consider a child’s understanding and the authority of anyone acting for them. We may need proportionate information to verify a request; please do not send identity documents unless we explain why they are necessary.

We cannot retrieve or remotely edit learning data that only exists on your device. You can change available settings in the app. Removing the app or clearing its website data can remove local information; see the support page before doing this, because saved work may be lost.

We normally respond to rights requests within one month, subject to the lawful exceptions and extensions. You can complain to us at support@bluffle.co.uk and to the Information Commissioner’s Office in the UK, or your relevant local data protection authority. You do not have to contact us first.

Children and grown-ups

The app is designed for ages 5–11, with grown-up help for setup and review. A grown-up check helps separate settings from play; it is not identity verification or a substitute for any consent required by law. Parents, carers and children can read the short privacy explanation together. Children should ask a grown-up to contact support.

Changes to this information

The date above identifies this notice. We will update it if the way we handle information changes and provide additional notice where required. A future feature that changes data handling needs its own assessment and clear information before use.
