THE FINAL LAUNCH CHALLENGE

All twelve facts.
A real reason to celebrate.

When the five activities are prepared, the final challenge asks all twelve facts for that times table in a random order. Every answer must be correct to launch the rocket and bring its crew home.

A new order keeps the focus on knowing the facts, rather than remembering a sequence.

A FEW LITTLE QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Can we download Multiploids yet?+
The public App Store launch is coming soon. We’ll add a download link here once Apple has approved the app and its public listing is live.
How does a rocket launch?+
Five preparation activities contribute to each numbered rocket. The final launch challenge requires all twelve facts for that table answered correctly, in a random order.
How does the Starbase grow?+
The Starbase starts with two games and adds another for each different numbered rocket launched. Launching the same number again does not unlock an additional game.
Do the learning activities work offline?+
Yes. Core learning and built-in speech work offline in the installed app. Downloading the app and opening website support links require an internet connection. A browser version needs its files loaded before offline use.
Does progress follow us to another device?+
No. Learner progress is stored on the device. There are no learner accounts or automatic device syncing; browser and installed-app progress are separate.
Where can a grown-up get help?+
Visit Multiploids support or email support@bluffle.co.uk.
