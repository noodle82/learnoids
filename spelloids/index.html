A few little questions.

Does Spelloids work offline?+
The installed app includes the library and its recordings. New or parent-edited wording may need the free on-device speech preparation step. The browser version needs its files downloaded before offline use.
Does progress sync between devices?+
No. Progress is stored on the device. The installed app and the browser version have separate saved progress.
Do we need an account?+
No Learnoids account is needed, and there are no public player profiles.
Where can I get help?+
Visit our support page or email support@bluffle.co.uk.
