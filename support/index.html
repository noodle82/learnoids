Let’s try the easy things first.

I cannot hear the spelling words.+
Turn up the device’s media volume and check the app’s sound switch. In Spelloids, ask a grown-up to open Speech & offline help and check the week’s audio preparation. If introductions play but words do not, include that detail in your support email.
The photograph did not read correctly.+
Crop around the spelling list, leaving a little room around the letters. Avoid headings and surrounding objects. Tap Read the spellings, then check every extracted word before using it. Type or correct the words if needed; recognition is not guaranteed for every photo.
A meaning or sentence needs changing.+
In Spelloids, open Grown-ups and edit the meaning or example. Words needing a meaning or sentence are highlighted. Save your changes and finish the audio check before handing the app back.
My browser and installed app show different progress.+
They store their data separately. There is currently no Learnoids account or automatic sync between the website, installed app or another device.
How do I protect our saved progress?+
Install app updates over the existing app. Do not delete the app or clear website data as a troubleshooting step unless you intend to remove local progress. Device backups depend on your Apple settings and are not managed by Learnoids.
How can I remove information?+
For browser play, clearing that app website’s stored data removes local progress, cached speech and creations. On iPhone or iPad, deleting the installed app removes its local app data; offloading is different and may retain data. Take care: these actions can be permanent. Device or cloud backups may retain copies under your own backup settings. For support emails, contact us to request deletion.

A note about children’s information

Please ask a grown-up to contact us. We do not need a child’s full name, home address, school name or a photograph of the child to help.
