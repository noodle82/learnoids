COOKIES & DEVICE STORAGE

A little clarity
about what gets saved.

The information website and the playable apps work differently.

Last updated 16 November 2026

This information website

The Learnoids landing, support and policy pages do not set Learnoids cookies, use visitor analytics or save information in local storage. Fonts and images are hosted with the site, with no embedded social feeds or video players. Your browser may cache files as part of loading web pages. The hosting provider also receives technical requests, as explained in Privacy.

The browser apps

If you use a playable browser version of Spelloids or Multiploids, local storage remembers your profiles, learning progress and settings. Spelloids also uses a local database for scrapbook media and prepared speech. Browser cache storage keeps app files available for offline use. These are functional storage uses for the learning experience you request; they are not advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

The installed apps

The installed apps save their own local app data. They do not automatically share it with Safari or a different device. Apple’s system backups depend on your device settings.

Your control

You can use your browser or device settings to remove stored information. Doing so may permanently remove learning records, creations and cached audio. Please read support guidance before clearing anything. A cookie banner has not been added for optional tracking because this site does not use it. We will reassess this information if storage or tracking changes.
