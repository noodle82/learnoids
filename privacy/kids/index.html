Your pal is yours.

You can give your creature a fun name. Use a nickname for yourself—your whole name is not needed.

⌂

Your work stays here.

Your words, answers and creations are saved on the device you are using. Other players cannot look at them through Learnoids. Someone using the same device might be able to see them.

✦

No adverts following you.

We do not use your learning to choose adverts. There is no public chat or player page.

Aa

A photo of words, please.

If a grown-up takes a picture for Spelloids, it should show the spelling list. It does not need to show you, your school or your address.

?

You can ask.

If something worries you or you want information removed, ask a trusted grown-up to help. They can contact Mark at support@bluffle.co.uk. You do not need to email us yourself.

Visiting a website sends some computer information so the page can load. A grown-up can read the details in our full privacy information.
