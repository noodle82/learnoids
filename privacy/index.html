The short version

  • No Learnoids account is needed to play.
  • Learning progress and creations stay on the device in the current apps.
  • We do not sell children’s information or use adverts or advertising trackers.
  • Website delivery and support emails involve hosting and email providers.

Who is responsible?

Learnoids is operated by Mark Atkin, who is responsible for the personal information described here. For support, privacy questions or a rights request, email support@bluffle.co.uk. Please contact us as a grown-up and avoid sending children’s identifying details.

The app notices explain local learning information. The sections below explain website visits, support messages and rights across Learnoids.

Using this website

The Learnoids information website does not use advertising trackers, visitor analytics, third-party font services, embedded videos, account registration or contact forms. Fonts and pictures are served with the site. Following a link to Apple or another website takes you to that provider’s service and privacy arrangements.

The website is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. When you request a page, GitHub receives technical information needed to deliver and protect the service, including your IP address. GitHub states that Pages visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes. See GitHub Pages information and the GitHub privacy statement for its handling, retention and international processing. We do not use these hosting requests to build advertising profiles.

Ordinary browser caching may keep copies of website files. The information website does not set its own cookies or use local storage. This differs from the playable browser apps, which need local storage and cached assets to save progress and work offline. See Cookies & storage.

When you email us

We receive the address you write from, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate faults, handle privacy requests and protect the service. Please send only what is needed: an app version and device model are usually more useful than a child’s name or school.

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in providing support and operating a safe, reliable service, balanced against your rights. Where we must keep a record to meet a legal obligation, we rely on that obligation instead. Providing an email is optional, but we need a way to reply if you want an answer.

We retain correspondence while dealing with the enquiry and afterwards only for as long as reasonably needed for follow-up, fault investigation, security, complaints or legal obligations. We review retained correspondence and delete or minimise information that is no longer needed. Where a legal issue requires longer retention, we keep only what is relevant.

Email delivery and storage involve our email provider. Website hosting involves GitHub. Providers may process information outside the UK; their locations and safeguards depend on the service. Where we arrange a restricted transfer, appropriate UK transfer safeguards or an applicable adequacy arrangement are required. Contact us for details relevant to your enquiry. We do not sell support information, add children to mailing lists or use enquiries to target advertising. We may disclose information where necessary to comply with law or protect legal rights.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask to access, correct or erase personal information we hold, restrict its use, or receive a portable copy where the right applies. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. If we ever rely on consent for an optional activity, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

These rights belong to the child where the information concerns the child. We consider a child’s understanding and the authority of anyone acting for them. We may need proportionate information to verify a request; please do not send identity documents unless we explain why they are necessary.

We cannot retrieve or remotely edit learning data that only exists on your device. You can change available settings in the app. Removing the app or clearing its website data can remove local information; see the support page before doing this, because saved work may be lost.

We normally respond to rights requests within one month, subject to the lawful exceptions and extensions. You can complain to us at support@bluffle.co.uk and to the Information Commissioner’s Office in the UK, or your relevant local data protection authority. You do not have to contact us first.

Changes to this information

The date above identifies this notice. We will update it if the way we handle information changes and provide additional notice where required. A future feature that changes data handling needs its own assessment and clear information before use.
