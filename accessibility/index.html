A warm welcome.

We want Learnoids to be readable, understandable and comfortable to use.

Last updated 16 November 2026

This website

The site uses text-based navigation, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings and labelled links. Pictures have descriptions where they add meaning. It adapts to smaller screens and supports enlarged text. Motion is reduced when your device requests reduced motion. There is no autoplaying audio or timed form to complete.

The apps

The apps combine sound, text and visual activities. Some hands-on activities and character customisation need visual interaction and may not be fully usable with a screen reader. We do not claim that every app activity meets a specific accessibility standard. Device and browser support may vary.

Tell us what would help

Email support@bluffle.co.uk with the page or activity, device, browser and assistive technology if relevant. Tell us what was difficult and what alternative would help. You do not need to share a diagnosis or a child’s identifying information.
